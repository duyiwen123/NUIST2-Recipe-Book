# Tomato and Egg Stir-Fry recipe

## Ingredients

- **3** large eggs
- **2** ripe tomatoes
- **1/2 teaspoon** salt
- **1/2 teaspoon** sugar
- **2 tablespoons** cooking oil
- **1** spring onion, chopped (optional)
- **A few drops** of sesame oil (optional)

## Instructions

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl, add a pinch of salt and beat them until the yolks and whites are well mixed.
2. Cut the tomatoes into wedges.
3. Heat the oil in a wok or frying pan over medium-high heat. Pour in the eggs and scramble them until just set, then scoop them out onto a plate.
4. Add a little more oil if needed, tip in the tomatoes and cook for a couple of minutes until they soften and release their juice.
5. Season with salt and sugar, then return the scrambled eggs to the pan.
6. Toss everything together for about half a minute, sprinkle with spring onion and a drop of sesame oil if using, and serve hot.
